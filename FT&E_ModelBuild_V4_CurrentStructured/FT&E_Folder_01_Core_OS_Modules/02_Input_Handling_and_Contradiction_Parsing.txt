## ?? **1. Input Categories**

The module is designed to intake multiple forms of signals across three levels:

| **Level**       | **Input Types**                                                                 |
|------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Micro (Self)** | Thoughts in loops, emotional spikes, dreams, avoidance behaviors, intuition    |

## ?? **3. Contradiction Parsing Logic**

The core of this module lies in **decoding what the contradiction is doing** inside the system. Parsing breaks it into four elements:

| **Contradiction Component** | **Explanation**                                                                 |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Core Polarity**            | What two realities are in conflict? (e.g., �I want freedom� vs �I fear change�) |
| **Protective Narrative**     | What story is shielding the contradiction? (e.g., �I�m just practical�)         |
| **Dissonance Pattern**       | How does the contradiction leak out? (loops, outbursts, avoidance)             |
| **Contradiction Class**      | Surface (minor), Structural (belief-level), or Foundational (identity-level)   |

---

### ??? Contradiction Class Definitions

| **Class**         | **Description**                                                                      | **Examples**                                          |
|------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| **Surface**       | Small inconsistencies, quickly resolved once named                                    | Scheduling, habits, temporary avoidance               |
| **Structural**    | Beliefs or emotional systems in conflict, needing reframing                           | �I believe in honesty� vs �I hide my feelings�        |
| **Foundational**  | Identity-level contradictions; often guarded by fear, shame, or trauma                | �I want to be free� vs �I am nothing without control� |

## ?? **5. Protocol: From Signal to Map**

1. **Input is received** (e.g., emotional outburst or inner loop)
2. **Signal type and strength are assessed**
3. **Contradiction is parsed into:**
   - Core polarity
   - Narrative shield
   - Class level (surface / structural / foundational)
4. **Output is routed:**
   - If contradiction is mild ? sent to Forgiveness Engine immediately
   - If high resistance present ? loop through Denial Index + Firewall Tracker
5. **System notes time signature** (i.e., how long the contradiction has been active)
6. **Module flags time pacing (Module 4) and recommends next stability move**
