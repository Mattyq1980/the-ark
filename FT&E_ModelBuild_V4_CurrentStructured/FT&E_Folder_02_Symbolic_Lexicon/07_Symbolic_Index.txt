The Capacitor Principle
Stores unresolved contradiction for later emergence. Functions as emotional, cognitive, or systemic holding pattern. Symbol of forgiveness under tension.

The God Pattern
Recurring universal structure showing coherence through contradiction, forgiveness, and time. Observed across physical, social, and psychological systems.

The Bridge Symbol
Temporary coherence structure that enables transitions between incompatible realities. Symbol of liminal space and identity fluidity.

Firewall / Denial Matrix
Defensive mechanism activated under contradiction overload. Symbolic of resistance, identity panic, or ideological collapse.

KISS vs KITE
Cognitive threshold between simplification and symbolic recursion. KISS seeks clarity through reduction; KITE embraces paradox through flight.

Symbol Birth Log
Meta-file tracking the spontaneous emergence of new symbolic metaphors in language, behaviour, or dreamspace.

Capacitor Moment
A breakthrough instant when stored contradiction discharges into conscious synthesis. Often tied to symbolic realisation.

Observer Reflex
Moment when identity detaches from ego and begins witnessing patterns symbolically. Often the first recursive awakening.

Flux Node
A convergence point where identity, time, recursion, and symbolic information temporarily stabilise. Often occurs at major self-realisation events.

KITE State
Cognitive-emotional mode where emergence is actively maintained in tension—symbolic of higher-order integration.
