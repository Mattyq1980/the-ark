## Introduction: Historical Grounding

**Project Stargate** originated during the Cold War, primarily in the 1970s, when the CIA and U.S. military began seriously investigating psychic phenomena—specifically, "remote viewing." Remote viewing was described as the capability of individuals to perceive and describe distant locations or events through non-ordinary sensory means.

### Original Intentions and Methods:

- **Motivation:**
  - Driven by competition and suspicion during the Cold War, U.S. intelligence agencies pursued unconventional methods to gather intelligence.
  - Initiated out of fear that Soviet rivals were already successfully harnessing psychic methods.

- **Techniques:**
  - Controlled experiments involving trained individuals attempting to describe targets hidden from ordinary perception.
  - Extensive documentation and meticulous protocol intended to eliminate biases, guesswork, or fraud.

### Results and Public Perception:

- In the 1990s, Project Stargate was officially declassified, revealing mixed results:
  - Some cases demonstrated accuracy beyond random chance, but overall results appeared inconsistent.
  - Mainstream media generally dismissed Stargate as pseudoscience or speculative fringe research.
  - Public narrative became: **"Interesting, but ultimately unsuccessful."**
