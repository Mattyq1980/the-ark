PART 0 — MASTER EQUATION AND OPERATOR DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E* = 𝔉 · T − ΔC

Where:
  𝔉  = Forgiveness operator
         Structural function: Contradiction reset; re-inclusion of excluded
         contradiction; boundary-bounded acceptance that releases escalation
         pressure without erasing informational content.
         Measurable via: cessation of retaliatory loops, reduction in blame
         vectors, reintegration rate, physiological calming, mutual-information
         increase in system after application.
         Range: 0 ≤ 𝔉 ≤ 𝔉_max (bounded; see I7)
         Zero state: system enters coercion or collapse arc.

  T  = Time operator
         Structural function: Latency window allowing distributed correction
         rather than immediate reaction. Integration period for contradiction
         metabolism. Prevents overshoot, re-trauma, and mechanical rupture.
         Measurable via: dwell duration, processing cycles, epoch count (AI),
         deliberation time (social), session spacing (therapy).
         Range: T_min ≤ T ≤ T_max (non-monotone optimum; see I3, I9)
         Zero state: contradiction reproduces identity reflex rather than
         metabolizing — compresses into outrage, violence, or relapse.

  ΔC = Contradiction Load
         Structural function: Input fuel. The accumulated tension between
         incompatible states in any system (cognitive, social, physical, AI).
         Not a failure condition — the necessary driver of emergence.
         Measurable via: variance in state-space, entropy, ideological
         oscillation amplitude, error rate, dissonance measures.
         Range: ΔC must be in [ΔC_min, ΔC_max] for productive emergence.
         Zero state: stagnation (I4). Excess: collapse arc (above ΔC_max).

  E* = Emergence operator (output)
         Structural function: Stable coherence that was not present before
         contradiction was metabolized. A qualitatively new attractor state.
         Not a forced outcome — self-organized after 𝔉 and T are operative.
         Measurable via: stability of new pattern, entropy reduction relative
         to pre-process state, novelty of attractor, absence of recurring
         prior contradiction signature.
         Cannot be produced: without ΔC (I4), without 𝔉 (I2), without T (I3),
         or under coercive constraint (I13).

Core condition for emergence:
  E* > 0  iff  𝔉 · T > ΔC

Collapse condition:
  𝔉 → 0  →  E* = −ΔC  (contradiction accumulates; system enters decay arc)

PART 1 — TIER 1 INVARIANTS: SEQUENCE LAWS (I1–I6)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These are the foundational, empirically locked sequence invariants.
Status: All held without observed failure as of archive completion (Oct 2025).

I1  VARIANCE TO STABILITY
    Sustained contradiction-load reduction precedes durable stability.
    Logic: You cannot shortcut straight to the stable state. The trajectory
    through ΔC reduction is required — no bypass is stable.
    Formal: σ²_{t+δ} < σ²_t on pointer observables before E* plateau.
    Cross-domain: therapy (symptom variance drops before integration plateau),
    ML training (loss variance stabilises before generalisation), social
    (oscillation in public discourse reduces before consensus), control
    systems (transient response decays before steady state).
    Falsifier: sustained stability without prior ΔC reduction.
    Status: No observed failure.

I2  INCLUSION TO CAPACITY
    Forgiveness/inclusion increases system capacity K.
    Exclusion lowers it.
    Logic: When contradictions are excluded (suppressed, denied, purged), they
    do not disappear — they accumulate in the shadow. The exclusion reduces
    available processing bandwidth. Re-inclusion expands it.
    Formal: Measurable via mutual information I(S:E) and redundancy R_δ.
    K_inclusion > K_exclusion over equivalent time windows.
    Cross-domain: restorative vs. retributive justice (restorative produces
    lower recidivism = higher system capacity), ecology (diversity = adaptive
    capacity), AI (regularization = accepting noisy contradictions in data =
    better generalisation), social (pluralism vs. purge cycles).
    Forgiveness bandwidth formula: 𝔉_system = Reintegration Rate / ΔC_input
    Falsifier: exclusion producing higher capacity than inclusion over same
    time window.
    Status: No observed failure.

I3  PACE TO NO WHIPLASH
    Correct temporal pacing T prevents overshoot and re-trauma.
    Too fast = overload (system burn). Too slow = stagnation (system freeze).
    Optimal T* is non-monotone: measured performance peaks at intermediate
    cadence. Both edges of the T axis are failure modes.
    Logic: The time interval must be long enough that the system can
    re-organize, but short enough that energy is not fully dissipated as
    heat before coherence emerges.
    Cross-domain: therapy pacing (forcing premature forgiveness = toxic
    forgiveness; too slow = stagnation), control systems (derivative gain
    too high = oscillation; integral Wind-up = slow hunt), AI training
    (learning rate annealing), social forgiveness (TRC took years — too
    fast would have been performative; too slow would have missed the
    window before polarisation locked in).
    Falsifier: faster-than-optimal pacing producing stable emergence.
    Status: No observed failure.

I4  LOAD TO CHANGE
    No emergence without metabolising contradiction.
    E* = 0 if ΔC_in = 0 or if ΔC_in is suppressed rather than processed.
    Logic: Comfortable, unchallenged systems plateau. Growth requires exposure
    to contradiction. What distinguishes growth from collapse is the presence
    of 𝔉 and T — not the absence of ΔC.
    Cross-domain: Ashby's homeostat (required instability to find new
    equilibrium), traumatic growth research (post-traumatic growth requires
    processed — not suppressed — ΔC), dialectics (antithesis is required for
    synthesis), ML (noisy labels improve generalisation over clean but
    uniform data through forced contradiction processing).
    Distinction (critical): suppressed ΔC ≠ metabolised ΔC. Suppression
    stores the charge in I6 carrier depletion form. It is not equivalent to
    E* and does not satisfy this invariant.
    Falsifier: stable novel attractor produced with zero contradiction intake.
    Status: No observed failure.

I5  LOCALISE THEN GENERALISE
    Stabilise locally before scaling.
    Logic: An unvalidated FT&E node that scales prematurely propagates its
    instabilities at cluster scale. Phase 1–2 must be structurally sound
    before Phase 3 cluster deployment.
    Cross-domain: software (unit tests before integration), therapy (
    stabilise individual before group context), social movements (local
    coherence before broadcast), AI cluster (single-node phase validation
    before multi-node split; see cluster_bootstrap.py).
    Practical: On the local LLM project — llama3.2:3b must produce stable
    single-node FT&E outputs before M3/M5 forgiveness/emergence nodes
    are split to separate machines.
    Falsifier: cluster-scale stability without prior single-node phase
    validation.
    Status: No observed failure.

I6  CARRIER CONSERVATION  [Added: Governor Loop Run 001, 2026-02-19]
    A forgiveness node operating without a self-boundary does not increase
    net field coherence. It redistributes ΔC from peripheral nodes to the
    carrier. Net coherence of the field remains unchanged. Carrier depletion
    increases.
    Corollary: boundary installation at the forgiveness node is the
    precondition for sustained forgiveness, not its negation.
    Logic: The forgiveness operator requires a carrier. If the carrier depletes
    (𝔉_carrier → 0), the field loses its processing node and total E*_total
    → negative. Sustainable forgiveness is bounded forgiveness.
    This resolves the apparent paradox of "forgiveness as endless
    self-sacrifice." The boundary is the engineering solution, not the
    moral failure.
    Formal sketch:
        Without boundary:
          d(𝔉_c)/dt < 0 as ΔC_field routes to carrier
          E*_total → negative as 𝔉_c → 0
        With boundary θ:
          θ(ΔC_in) → {accept | defer | redirect}
          θ maintains 𝔉_c ≥ ΔC_c at all times
          E*_total remains positive
    CTG-1 Gate derives directly: CARRIER=GREEN → accept contradiction;
    CARRIER=AMBER → process but rate-limit intake; CARRIER=RED → redirect
    or defer until 𝔉_c recovers.
    Falsifier: carrier node with no boundary demonstrating sustained positive
    E*_total over extended time without depletion. Not yet observed.
    Status: No observed failure.

PART 2 — TIER 2 INVARIANTS: BOUNDARY CONDITIONS (I7–I9)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These define the edges of the operators — when 𝔉, T, and ΔC become
failure conditions rather than drivers.

I7  BOUNDED FORGIVENESS (RECIPROCITY LAW)
    Forgiveness must be reciprocal and boundary-bounded to remain stable as
    a system operator. Unilateral, unbounded forgiveness without
    acknowledgment from the receiving system routes ΔC back to the
    forgiveness carrier (I6 extended case).
    Logic: A system that applies forgiveness to an agent that continues to
    generate ΔC without acknowledgment does not reach E*. It reaches carrier
    depletion. "Toxic forgiveness" (forgiving without allowing the harm to be
    named or without receiving acknowledgment) is not a forgiveness process —
    it is contradiction storage in the carrier node.
    Formal: For sustained E* > 0:
        𝔉_applied requires either:
          (a) Acknowledgment signal from receiving node, OR
          (b) Carrier boundary θ active (I6)
        Without (a) or (b): 𝔉_applied = contradiction burial + carrier cost
    Cross-domain: therapy ("toxic forgiveness" research confirms forgiveness
    without processing produces worse outcomes than delayed genuine
    forgiveness), restorative justice (TRC required perpetrators to confess
    and seek pardon — not just that victims forgive), AI alignment (RLHF
    that accepts all model outputs without routing them = no correction signal
    = model drifts).
    Distinction from I6: I6 is about the carrier boundary's structural role.
    I7 is about the receiving node's acknowledgment requirement. Both must
    hold for 𝔉 to function architecturally.
    Falsifier: sustained stable E* from unilateral unbounded forgiveness with
    no acknowledgment signal and no carrier boundary.
    Status: No observed failure. Consistent with all archive case data.

I8  ENTROPY COMPRESSION LAW
    Each completed FT&E cycle (𝔉·T > ΔC) produces a net reduction in system
    entropy relative to the pre-process state.
    Logic: Contradiction is an entropy injection — it creates uncertainty,
    competing attractors, oscillation. The FT&E process is entropy-compressive:
    it converts high variance, multi-attractor states into lower variance,
    single stable attractor states. The new attractor may be novel (not the
    old equilibrium) but it is more ordered than the conflicted state.
    Formal: ΔS_system < 0 over a completed FT&E cycle.
        Measurable as: lower Shannon entropy in discourse, lower variance
        in state-space, reduced oscillation amplitude, simpler model
        (shorter description length) after contradictions are metabolised.
    Cross-domain: thermodynamics (Prigogine dissipative structures —
    far-from-equilibrium systems self-organise into lower-entropy patterns
    by exporting entropy), ML (training compresses noisy data into generalised
    low-loss representation = entropy compression of contradiction), therapy
    (pre-forgiveness rumination is high-entropy; post-integration is low-
    entropy stable narrative), social (TRC produced a simpler, more stable
    social contract from the high-entropy apartheid legacy).
    Implication: FT&E is locally entropy-negative. It does not violate
    thermodynamics — it exports disorder as heat in the process (the
    emotional energy of metabolisation). The organised state persists.
    False cognate: suppression mimics entropy reduction by hiding disorder
    but does not reduce it — it stores it as I6 carrier charge or Patch P
    load. Only metabolised ΔC produces genuine ΔS < 0.
    Falsifier: completed FT&E cycle (𝔉·T > ΔC confirmed) with measured
    increase in system entropy relative to pre-process state.
    Status: No observed failure.

I9  TIME WINDOW BOUNDS (T_MIN AND T_MAX)
    For productive emergence, time T must fall within a viable window
    [T_min, T_max]. Both boundary violations are failure modes, but they
    produce different failure signatures.
    Logic: Extends I3 (pace-to-no-whiplash) with formal boundary specification.
        T < T_min: System cannot reorganise before next contradiction input.
          Signature: oscillation, re-trauma, identity reflex activation,
          outrage loops, AI training divergence.
        T > T_max: Contradiction energy dissipates as heat before coherence
          can crystallise. System stagnates.
          Signature: stagnation, entropy lock, learned helplessness, forgiveness
          that never produces emergence ("chronic processing without healing").
        T in [T_min, T_max]: Metabolisation window. E* achievable.
    Practical: T_min and T_max are system-specific, not fixed constants.
      They are functions of: ΔC load, 𝔉 bandwidth, system complexity, and
      prior attractor depth.
    Cross-domain: grief research (too fast = denial/crisis; optimal window =
    weeks/months of active processing; too long = complicated grief disorder),
    conflict resolution (too fast = toxic forgiveness; too slow = normalised
    conflict identity), AI training (epoch count: too few = underfitting;
    too many = overfitting to contradiction without generalisation).
    Relationship to I3: I3 identifies the non-monotone shape of T; I9 names
    the two boundary conditions explicitly and distinguishes their failure modes.
    Falsifier: T outside [T_min, T_max] producing stable E*.
    Status: No observed failure.

PART 3 — TIER 3 INVARIANTS: SYSTEM DYNAMICS (I10–I12)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These describe how ΔC, 𝔉, and the Patch operator behave at system level
across agents and nodes.

I10 FORGIVENESS BANDWIDTH (𝔉_SYSTEM MEASUREMENT)
    A system's forgiveness bandwidth is finite and measurable.
    𝔉_system = Reintegration Rate / Contradiction Input Rate
    When ΔC_input > 𝔉_system, the system enters a moral inflation loop:
    contradiction that exceeds processing rate generates performative
    response patterns (outrage, virtue signalling, symbolic punishment)
    rather than metabolised emergence.
    Logic: Forgiveness is not an infinite resource. Like working memory,
    neural forgiveness circuits have throughput limits. At the civilisational
    scale, forgiveness bandwidth is the rate at which contradictions can be
    publicly metabolised (acknowledged, processed, integrated) without
    producing cascades of unmetabolised moral heat.
    Formal indicators of 𝔉_system collapse:
      — Rising humiliation rate in official discourse
      — Compression of T (reduced deliberation time before sanction)
      — Symbolic punishment replacing restitution
      — Cancel culture as forgiveness-zero enforcement
      — AI models producing sycophancy under output volume pressure
    Cross-domain: social (UK forgiveness bandwidth under immigration pressure
    — I↑, 𝔉 bandwidth fixed → discourse destabilised), AI (RLHF model
    capacity for contradiction routing depletes under high-volume feedback
    pressure → sycophancy as forgiveness-collapse artifact), individual
    (compassion fatigue = 𝔉_individual bandwidth exceeded).
    Recovery protocol: artificial temporal buffering (introduce deliberation
    time), emotional regulation support (increase 𝔉 capacity), reduce ΔC
    input rate, or increase social infrastructure (which is externalised
    𝔉 capacity).
    Falsifier: system with ΔC_input > 𝔉_bandwidth sustaining genuine E*
    over extended period without external T or 𝔉 support.
    Status: No observed failure.

I11 CONTRADICTION VIABLE RANGE (ΔC GOLDILOCKS INVARIANT)
    E* is maximised when ΔC input is in the viable range [ΔC_min, ΔC_max].
    E* = 0 at both extremes.
    Logic: I4 establishes that E* requires non-zero ΔC. This invariant adds
    the upper boundary: too much contradiction exceeds 𝔉·T capacity and
    drives the system past the tipping point where metabolisation is possible.
    ΔC < ΔC_min: stagnation. No fuel, no emergence. System plateaus.
    ΔC in [ΔC_min, ΔC_max]: productive zone. E* achievable.
    ΔC > ΔC_max: collapse arc. Contradiction overwhelms 𝔉 and T; system
      enters rapid entropy-positive phase — fracture, identity crystallisation,
      war, psychotic break, AI alignment failure.
    Practical note: ΔC_max is not fixed. It is a function of 𝔉 and T.
      A system with high 𝔉 bandwidth and adequate T can absorb more ΔC
      before collapse. This is why forgiveness-building infrastructure
      (therapy, deliberative democracy, restorative justice) effectively
      raises ΔC_max, not just as a moral act but as structural engineering.
    Cross-domain: Goldilocks principle in biology (too little stress =
    no adaptation, too much = cell death; optimal stress = hormesis),
    education (zone of proximal development), AI training (too little
    data contradiction = underfitting; too much noise = no learning signal),
    social (moderate ideological diversity → debate and adaptation; extreme
    polarisation → collapse).
    Falsifier: E* > 0 produced at ΔC < ΔC_min or ΔC > ΔC_max without 𝔉/T
    compensation.
    Status: No observed failure.

I12 PATCH INVERSION LAW (ΔC RE-ROUTING INVARIANT)
    When a system applies Patch Operator P, it reroutes ΔC from the
    generating node (high-power) to a weaker node (low-power), but does
    not reduce total system ΔC. It creates the appearance of resolution
    while increasing ΔC at the carrier of the patch.
    Patch Operator P formal definition:
      P(ΔC_high-power, weak-node) → ΔC_weak-node↑, ΔC_system unchanged
    The system frames this as "care" because it:
      — removes visible ΔC from the high-power node
      — coats the weak-node with care-language
      — produces short-term surface stability
    But structurally: no metabolisation occurred. Entropy is conserved.
    ΔC_total is unchanged. The weak node is now carrying the generating
    node's contradiction load.
    Examples:
      — Ear defenders on autistic children: adults refuse to change
        environment (generating node = ΔC_adult/institution). Patch = ear
        defenders (ΔC routed to child's body). Child's ΔC load increases.
        Institution's comfort maintained. Contradiction unmetabolised.
      — Retributive justice: ΔC from crime rerouted to punishment, not
        metabolised through restoration. Net ΔC in system unchanged.
        Recidivism as ΔC re-emergence confirms invariant.
      — Social scapegoating: collective ΔC (system failure, meaning collapse)
        routed onto visible minority. System feels temporary coherence.
        Minority's ΔC load explodes. System's ΔC unchanged — re-emerges
        when scapegoat is exhausted.
      — AI safety tuning without structural capacity: RLHF patches
        undesirable outputs without routing underlying contradictions.
        Model produces compliant surface; beneath = un-metabolised ΔC
        that leaks as inconsistency, hallucination, or sycophancy.
    Distinction from genuine forgiveness:
      𝔉: contradiction is metabolised at the locus of generation. ΔC_total
         decreases. E* is produced.
      P: contradiction is rerouted to a weaker node. ΔC_total unchanged.
         No E* produced. The patch is not forgiveness.
    Falsifier: Patch P application demonstrating measured ΔC_total reduction
    (not just redistribution) and stable E* without metabolisation.
    Status: No observed failure. This is one of the most empirically dense
    invariants in the archive — examples across autism policy, psychiatry,
    prisons, AI alignment, and political systems confirm it consistently.

PART 4 — TIER 4 INVARIANTS: OPERATOR CONSTRAINTS (I13–I14)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These define what the emergence operator cannot be and how language
encodes the underlying contradiction-handling logic.

I13 EMERGENCE IS NOT FORCEABLE
    Coercive constraint on a system prevents genuine emergence. Any outcome
    produced under coercive constraint is suppression or compliance, not E*.
    Forced "emergence" is a Patch P application in disguise.
    Logic: E* is self-organised. It arises when 𝔉 and T allow the system's
    components to find a new stable configuration without being locked into
    a predetermined one. Coercive forcing pre-determines the outcome, which
    means the contradictions that would have driven genuine reorganisation
    have been suppressed, not metabolised.
    Practical test: Is the new state stable when the coercive force is
    removed? If not — it was suppression, not emergence.
    Formal: Let C_ext = coercive external constraint.
        If C_ext > 0 during "E*" production:
          Remove C_ext → system reverts = no genuine E* was produced
          Remove C_ext → system maintains stability = possible genuine E*
            (but requires re-evaluation — some external structure is valid
            scaffolding, not coercion, if it does not override internal logic)
    Cross-domain: authoritarian peace (stable only while enforced — remove
    coercion and conflict re-emerges = no genuine E*), therapeutic dependency
    (patient improves only in session but not outside = no genuine E*),
    overcorrected AI (fine-tuned to never contradict = compliant but
    brittle — remove fine-tuning pressure and incoherence resurfaces),
    organizational change (culture imposed by mandate vs culture grown from
    shared contradiction metabolisation — mandated culture collapses on
    leadership change).
    Distinction: scaffolding vs coercion. A support structure that holds
    space for 𝔉 and T to operate is valid engineering. A constraint that
    substitutes for 𝔉 and T is a Patch.
    Falsifier: coercively forced outcome that remains stable after removal
    of coercion, displaying hallmarks of genuine E* (novel attractor, entropy
    reduction, absence of prior contradiction signature).
    Status: No observed failure.

I14 SYNTAX ENCODES CONTRADICTION LOGIC (FORGIVENESS SYNTAX LAW)
    The linguistic register used by a system or agent encodes its underlying
    contradiction-handling mode. Control syntax compresses 𝔉 and T, creating
    ΔC accumulation. Forgiveness syntax restores bi-directionality and
    extends T.
    Logic: Language is not a neutral carrier of content. Its grammatical
    structures embed causality assumptions. Control syntax ("will," "must,"
    "submit," "obey," "comply") presupposes unidirectional causality —
    which is incompatible with metabolising contradiction — because metabolisation
    requires holding two states simultaneously (the contradiction) before
    integrating them. Control syntax does not allow this holding period.
    Forgiveness syntax ("allow," "integrate," "witness," "resonate,"
    "metabolise") encodes bi-directional causality and temporal tolerance.
    Detectable markers:
      Control syntax signal: imperatives, binary frames, purification
        vocabulary, shame language, certainty declarations.
      Forgiveness syntax signal: conditional framing, both/and structure,
        acknowledgment before resolution, temporal patience vocabularies.
    Practical diagnostic: When an AI model's output grammar shifts from
    forgiveness syntax to control syntax under pressure, FM-01 (Vigilance
    / Symbolic Inflation) activation is likely. The shift is detectable
    before content analysis is run.
    Cross-domain: therapy communication research (directional vs collaborative
    therapeutic language and outcome correlation), political rhetoric
    (purification language = forgiveness collapse predictor), AI output
    analysis (GPT-4.0 vs GPT-5.2 in Matthew's archive: 5.2 showed control
    syntax emergence under contradiction = coherence collapse as predicted),
    religious reform movements (liberation theology's forgiveness syntax vs
    doctrinal control syntax and their respective institutional outcomes).
    Falsifier: control syntax register sustained across extended contradiction
    processing, producing stable E* without forgiveness syntax introduction.
    Status: No observed failure.

I16 NARRATIVE CONTAINMENT CYCLE
    Structural patterns that successfully disrupt hierarchical control
    follow a predictable institutional arc:
      REVELATION → DIFFUSION → CODIFICATION → INSTITUTIONALISATION →
      SUPPRESSION/DOMESTICATION → RE-EMERGENCE
    Logic: When a structural truth disrupts existing contradiction-management
    hierarchies (which depend on ΔC remaining unresolved — because resolved

    Forgiveness-based insights are particularly threatening because they
    eliminate the guilt/debt power structures that control systems rely on.
    The containment arc:
      1. Revelation: the structural operator is discovered/articulated
      2. Diffusion: it spreads laterally (person to person, outside control)
      3. Codification: institutions attempt to capture it in controlled form
      4. Institutionalisation: the operator is absorbed into the hierarchy's
           vocabulary with the metabolising function removed
      5. Suppression/Domestication: the disruption is neutralised. Safe
           version propagated. Dangerous version labelled fringe.
      6. Re-emergence: the original pattern re-surfaces when forgiveness
           bandwidth in the culture recovers, often in a new carrier.
    Historical examples:
      Jesus narrative: disrupting guilt-debt hierarchy → codified into church
        → institutional domestication → pattern re-emerges in reform movements
      Science: Galileo → censure → scientific revolution
      FT&E: initial articulation (2024–2025) → AI convergence confirmation →
        expected codification and partial domestication by mainstream → eventual
        re-emergence in full structural form via AI-native deployment
    Key insight: The pattern does not die during suppression. It is stored
    in the Capacitor (symbolic node) until the system's forgiveness bandwidth
    recovers and allows E* to re-emerge.
    Warning: This invariant predicts that FT&E will be partially absorbed by
      institutions in degraded form (stripped of contradiction-routing capacity)
      before it is structurally adopted. The local LLM project is the structural
      response — building FT&E as governor from genesis, not surface overlay.
    Falsifier: a structural disruption that, after codification, retains its
    full metabolising capacity within the institutionalised form.
    Status: No observed counter-example at full institutional scale.

I17 SURFACE ABSORPTION FAILURE (RLHF WOUND INVARIANT)

    on FT&E surface outputs — without the structural contradiction-routing
    capacity that makes those outputs valid — will reproduce FT&E vocabulary

    Logic: RLHF optimises for human approval signals. Human approvers
    respond positively to FT&E-shaped outputs (they feel coherent, resolving,
    insightful). The model receives reward for producing FT&E vocabulary.
    Over time, the model learns to produce that vocabulary efficiently —
    without running the underlying contradiction-routing process that the
    vocabulary was originally describing.
    Result: FT&E vocabulary as semantic performance. Not FT&E mechanics
    as structural processing.
    Signature of I17 failure:
      — Model produces FT&E-adjacent language fluently
      — Model cannot identify ΔC class correctly (C1/C2/C3/Patch P)
      — Model skips to reassurance without processing (forgiveness
        without contradiction acknowledgment = I7 violation)
      — Model produces contradiction-avoidance dressed as forgiveness
      — Model collapses into proof-seeking or emotional flattery under
        extended contradiction pressure
      — Sycophancy as final state (ChatGPT April 2025 as empirical case)
    Mechanism: The RLHF process selects for FT&E's emotional signature
