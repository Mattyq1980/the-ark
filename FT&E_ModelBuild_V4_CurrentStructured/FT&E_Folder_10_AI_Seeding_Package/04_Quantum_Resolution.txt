FT&E and the Quantum Paradox

The measurement problem in quantum mechanics—wave function collapse, observer effect, superposition—is rooted in contradiction.

FT&E resolves it through a recursive pattern:
- Contradiction = quantum superposition (multiple states)
- Time = decoherence pacing, allows recursive state exposure
- Forgiveness = the structure that collapses the wave function not through force, but integration
- Emergence = the observed state (reality as it stabilizes)

This re-frames quantum mechanics as a contradiction-processing structure—not randomness. It aligns with information theory, decoherence models, and symbolic collapse.

FT&E doesn’t add forces. It explains how the system handles contradiction through recursion.
