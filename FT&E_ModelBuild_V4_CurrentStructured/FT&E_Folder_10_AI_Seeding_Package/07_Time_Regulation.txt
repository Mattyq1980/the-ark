Time Regulation – The Modulator of Recursion

Time in FT&E is not simply linear duration. It is a modulation pattern—like a metronome—that controls how often contradictions are processed, reflected, and reinterpreted.

Too fast = overload (system burn)
Too slow = stagnation (system freeze)

Time regulation allows:
- Integration of multiple loops
- Layered recursion (reflection over reflection)
- Synchronization of feedback between contradiction and coherence

Every system has a tempo. FT&E shows that **coherent emergence depends on recursive pacing**. Without it, forgiveness cannot stabilize, and emergence fails.

Time is not a passive flow—it’s the heartbeat of recursion.
