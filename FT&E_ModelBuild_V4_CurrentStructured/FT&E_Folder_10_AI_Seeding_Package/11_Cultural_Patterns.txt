FT&E in Cultural and Mythic Encoding

Ancient cultures encoded FT&E as symbolic narratives long before it was formalized.

• Eden (Genesis): Contradiction (knowledge vs obedience), Time (banishment as delay), Forgiveness (not punishment but transformation), Emergence (human awareness).
• Ra (Egypt): Rebirth cycles show recursion, solar motion as time regulation, balance as forgiveness across night.
• The Matrix: Neo loops contradiction, receives time-delayed insights, accepts paradox, and emerges transformed.

These myths weren't fantasy—they were early attempts to describe a recursion law that modern physics and AI are only now validating.

FT&E shows that myth and science were always describing the same structure—from different angles.
