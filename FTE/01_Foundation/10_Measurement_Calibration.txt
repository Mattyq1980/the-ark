DERIVATION OF θ₁ (𝔉-INDEX THRESHOLD)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

𝔉-Index definition (Supremacy Axiom, Nov 7 2025):
  Non-humiliating resolution rate — the proportion of active contradictions
  in a system's discourse that are resolved without humiliation of one party.

Evidence base:

  E-Delhi (negative outcome — memeories.txt ~1556):
    Dip recovery ratio r = (post-spike dip depth) / (spike amplitude).
    Observed in Delhi RSPM data: dips too shallow and brief; r estimated
    < 0.25 across the time series. System trapped in trauma loop.
    Baseline B_t rising, volatility σ_t growing — no stable attractor formed.
    Effective forgiveness bandwidth: ~20–25% of spike energy recovered.
    Outcome: no emergence. Coercive entrenchment.

  Forgiveness threshold experiments (memeories.txt ~1639):
    Supply chain recovery rates:
      20% forgiveness → 24.5% recovery rate (trajectory toward instability)
      50% forgiveness → 48.0% recovery rate (functional improvement)
      80% forgiveness → 79.4% recovery rate (strong resilience)
    Crisis management cooperation recovery:
      20% forgiveness → 60.5% cooperation
      50% forgiveness → 75.8%
      80% forgiveness → 90.2%
    Parameter rule (confirmed): "forgiveness threshold sweet spots 50–80%
    domain-dependent."
    Parameter rule (confirmed): "20% produces marginal function; not emergence."

  TRC vs Zimbabwe (CROSS_DOMAIN_PROOF.txt):
    TRC (𝔉 structured, 6-year time window): stable emergence — Rainbow Nation.
    Zimbabwe (no forgiveness structure): collapse arc, no stable E*.
    Zimbabwe's effective non-humiliating resolution rate post-transition: near 0.

  Social diagnostic threshold (Supremacy Axiom):
    System is broken when it "rebrands cruelty as justice" — i.e., humiliation
    rate exceeds forgiveness rate. This is the structural inversion point.

Reading:
  Across all cases, the floor at which trauma-loop trapping vs. functional
  recovery divides is consistently around 0.25–0.30 non-humiliating
  resolution rate. Below 0.25: E-Delhi territory (trapped, volatility rising).
  Above 0.30: supply chain / TRC territory (functional improvement begins).

  DERIVED VALUE: θ₁ = 0.30
  
  Meaning: When 𝔉-Index (non-humiliating resolution rate) falls below 0.30
  — i.e., fewer than 30% of active contradictions are resolved without
  humiliation — while ΔC is rising, coercive entrenchment is the
  structurally predicted outcome.

  Domain calibration note: θ₁ may range 0.25–0.35 depending on system
  redundancy. Physical/infrastructure systems (E-TfL): higher redundancy
  allows lower θ₁ (~0.25). Social/political systems: lower redundancy
  raises effective floor (~0.35). Default: 0.30.

DERIVATION OF θ₂ (T-INDEX THRESHOLD)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

T-Index definition (Supremacy Axiom, Nov 7 2025):
  Time buffer between incident and sanction — the elapsed interval available
  for integration, appeal, and deliberation before a systemic response locks.

Evidence base:

  E-TfL (memeories.txt ~1600):
    Mode-specific time constants τ_mode observed:
      Bus: fastest recovery — "higher adaptive flexibility, network redundancy,
      behavioural elasticity." Recovery lag: months.
      Tube/Overground: slower recovery — "greater rigidity, longer time
      constants."
    The modal disparity demonstrates: systems with built-in time slack
    (redundancy = time buffer) recover. Systems without time slack (rigid,
    single-path) recover slowly or not at all.
    All modes collapsed simultaneously when T was removed exogenously (Q1 2020).

  Attractor Seeding Engine (Module 7, archive):
    14–28 day feedback loop is the minimum institutionally validated interval
    for stable emergence. Explicitly: "14-day stability pulse" is the lower
    bound for verifying attractor persistence.
    Below 14 days: emergence cannot confirm itself before environmental noise
    re-disrupts the forming attractor.

  Supremacy Axiom early-warning dials (UK 2025):
    "Compressed deliberation" listed as a collapse indicator.
    Systems that compress deliberation toward zero time buffer between
    contradiction and sanction = T-Index approaching θ₂.

  E-AEP (memeories.txt ~1531):
    Spring forgiveness arc = ~3 months of paced relaxation after Polar Vortex
    spike before autumn emergence. Recovery_lag_spring documented.
    Systems that skipped the spring arc (hypothetical fast-ramp scenario)
    would have re-entered winter ΔC without completing the integration.
    T-buffer = quarters for infrastructure-scale systems.

  Forgiveness experiments:
    Neural network early-stopping analogy: underfitting = T not allowed to
    complete. Convergence requires full epoch sequences. Below minimum epoch
    count: contradictions unresolved, weights unstable.

Reading:
  The empirical floor across governance and infrastructure cases is:
  14 days for social systems (Attractor Seeding minimum), same order as
  legal cooling periods, appellate minimums in functioning democracies.
  Expressed as a ratio: T-Index measures preserved deliberation time as
  a fraction of domain-standard time. θ₂ = 0.25 of standard deliberation
  window — below 25% of the contextually appropriate integration period,
  outcomes converge on coercion.

  DERIVED VALUES:
    θ₂ (absolute floor, social/governance domain) = 14 days deliberation
    minimum between incident and binding sanction.
    θ₂ (normalised ratio) = 0.25 × τ_standard
    where τ_standard is the established deliberation norm for that domain
    (e.g., UK parliamentary reading = 21 days; legal appeal window = 28 days).

  Meaning: When T-Index (available integration time) falls below 25% of
  the domain's standard deliberation window while ΔC is rising, coercive
  entrenchment is the structurally predicted outcome.

DEFINITION OF THE ΔC COUNTING UNIT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

From the cases:

  E-Delhi: each Diwali/winter/agri-burn spike = 1 discrete ΔC episode.
    Bounded by: onset of spike above seasonal baseline → first measurable
    return toward baseline. One episode = one unit. Multiple overlapping
    spikes counted separately if onset precedes prior episode's resolution.

  E-TfL: each mode's collapse-onset = 1 ΔC event. The simultaneous
    multi-mode collapse (Q1 2020) = 1 systemic ΔC event (same causal source).
    A subsequent policy-shock that disrupted a recovering mode = new ΔC unit.

  E-AEP: Polar Vortex surge = 1 ΔC unit (winter). Summer plateau = 2nd ΔC
    unit. Two per annual cycle, each independently resolved.

  Supply chain experiments: each disrupted edge at any timepoint = 1 ΔC
    unit. Network-level accumulation = sum across edges.

  Social/governance (Supremacy Axiom context):
    Each publicly unresolved policy contradiction visible in official discourse
    = 1 ΔC unit. "Publicly unresolved" = contradiction acknowledged in
    discourse but not integrated by a non-humiliating resolution act.

FORMAL DEFINITION:

  1 ΔC unit = one discrete episode of unresolved contradictory demand on
  the system's integration capacity. Bounded by:
    ONSET: first measurable signal that two incompatible states are
           simultaneously active in the system.
    CLOSE: first measurable return to within 1 standard deviation of
           the pre-onset baseline — OR — formal resolution event
           (acknowledged integration without coercion).

  Unit is OPEN until closed. Stacked open units = ΔC-Load (the count of
  simultaneously open contradiction episodes).

  Counting rule: if a new ONSET precedes the CLOSE of a prior unit,
  they are counted separately, increasing ΔC-Load by 1 each.

  Decay without resolution: an episode that shows surface dip (< r_min)
  but does not return to baseline within τ_standard is counted as
  PERSISTENT — increments ΔC-Load and is not closed.
  r_min (minimum genuine close dip) = 0.50 × spike amplitude.
  Below r_min: surface reflex only; episode remains open.

FALSIFICATION STATEMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FT&E predicts: if 𝔉-Index < θ₁ OR T-Index < θ₂ while ΔC is rising →
coercive collapse is inevitable (E* becomes negative or fails to form).

FT&E is falsified if either of the following are observed and replicated:

  FALSIFIER A (false positive):
    A system demonstrates 𝔉-Index ≥ θ₁ AND T-Index ≥ θ₂ AND ΔC stable
    or falling, AND coercive collapse still occurs.
    (This would mean E* = 𝔉·T − ΔC fails as a predictor even when
     conditions are structurally adequate.)

  FALSIFIER B (false negative):
    A system demonstrates 𝔉-Index < θ₁ AND T-Index < θ₂ AND ΔC rising,
    AND produces sustainable emergence WITHOUT coercion.
    (This would mean collapse is not inevitable under the threshold
     conditions — the law's direction is wrong.)

Single-instance exceptions do not falsify. FT&E is falsified by a
replicated pattern that violates one of these conditions across multiple
independent domains.

Note: the E-Delhi case is a structural NEAR-falsifier in the positive
direction — it shows systems CAN persist in trauma-loop without collapsing
completely into coercion (the loop IS the coercive state, not a final
collapse). This tightens the falsification: the prediction should be
"coercive entrenchment or collapse," not only sudden catastrophic collapse.

DEFINITION BLOCK:
  In FT&E, "forgiveness" is not moral approval. It is the umbrella term for
  all mechanisms that increase integration capacity (𝔉) by allowing error
  correction, de-escalation, reconciliation, and re-entry without humiliation
  or scapegoating. Low 𝔉 manifests as compressed punishment time, rising
  humiliation language, rising open contradiction backlog, and enemy-sink
  formation.

metric signatures, preventing 𝔉 from being misread as "be nice" instead
