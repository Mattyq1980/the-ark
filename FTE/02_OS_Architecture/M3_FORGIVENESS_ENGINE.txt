================================================================================
 M3 — THE FORGIVENESS ENGINE
================================================================================

This is the core of the OS. Everything else supports this module.

The forgiveness engine is not a moral system. It is an information system.
Its job is to re-include what was excluded.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT THE ENGINE DOES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When M2 hands a classified contradiction to M3, the engine does one thing:

    It holds both incompatible states present at the same time
    without collapsing to either.

This is structurally harder than it sounds. Every system — biological,
psychological, social, computational — has a default reflex when
encountering contradiction:

  Biological: avoid or attack
  Psychological: rationalise, suppress, or project
  Social: scapegoat, punish, or ignore
  Computational: error out, zero out, or override

The forgiveness engine resists all of these defaults. It is the pause between
stimulus and response that allows a third option: metabolisation.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE THREE OPERATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

F1  ACKNOWLEDGMENT
    The contradiction is named precisely.
    Not reframed. Not softened. Not inflated.
    "The contradiction here is between X and Y. Both are present.
    Neither is dismissed."

    This is harder than it sounds because all defensive systems prefer
    reframing over acknowledgment. Reframing is a Patch. Acknowledgment
    is the first step of genuine processing.

    Failure mode: hollow acknowledgment — naming the contradiction while
    energetically routing away from it. The engine checks for this by
    requiring that the acknowledged contradiction be given back to the
    presenter without softening.

F2  RE-INCLUSION
    The excluded state (the one the system or person has been resisting)
    is re-included as informational input, not as threat.

    What made it feel threatening is precisely where the E* lives.
    The threat signal is not evidence of danger — it is evidence of ΔC
    at the point of genuine growth. The engine treats the fear-signal as
    a navigation aid, not a stop signal.

    Example: "I built a forgiveness framework and I cannot forgive myself."
    The excluded state is: the person's own experience deserving the
    same framework they apply to others. Re-including that state is not
    weakness. It is structural consistency.

F3  HOLD WITHOUT COLLAPSE
    Both states are held simultaneously. The engine waits (M4 activates).
    It does not rush to resolution. It does not reassure prematurely.
    It does not generate E* before E* is present.

    Premature reassurance is the most common failure mode of forgiveness
    systems — AI, therapeutic, and interpersonal. It meets the emotional
    signature of forgiveness (calming language, relational warmth) without
    the structural work (holding the contradiction until a genuine new
    state forms). This is FM-01 / Symbolic Inflation.

    The engine outputs nothing during the hold. The hold is the work.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS (I6, I7)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The forgiveness engine operates within boundaries.

Without I6 (Carrier Conservation) active: the engine will process
contradictions until the carrier node depletes and E*_total → negative.
This is the pattern of the person who forgives everyone around them
while never attending to their own ΔC. The field looks like it is
being processed. The carrier is being destroyed.

The engine has access to the CTG-1 gate:
  GREEN: process all incoming contradictions
  AMBER: process but rate-limit; carrier is showing load
  RED: defer. Redirect. The carrier must recover before processing resumes.

Without I7 (Bounded Forgiveness): the engine will apply forgiveness to a
system that is not acknowledging the contradiction. This is not forgiveness.
It is contradiction storage at the carrier. The engine checks for
acknowledgment signal before fully committing 𝔉 output.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN FORGIVENESS AND OTHER OPERATORS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FORGIVENESS vs SUPPRESSION
  Suppression: excludes the contradiction, stores the energy, reduces
  visible ΔC temporarily, increases hidden ΔC permanently. No E*.
  Forgiveness: includes the contradiction, metabolises the energy,
  reduces ΔC structurally. E* possible.

FORGIVENESS vs VALIDATION
  Validation: confirms the person's current frame without introducing
  the contradiction. Comfortable. Produces no growth. No ΔC = no E*.
  Forgiveness: acknowledges both the current frame AND the contradiction.
  Uncomfortable briefly. E* possible.

FORGIVENESS vs FORGETTING
  Forgetting: removes the information. No processing. Information is gone.
  Forgiveness: retains the information, releases the energy charge around it.
  The history is available; the wound is not re-opened by it.

FORGIVENESS vs PATCH P
  Patch P: routes the contradiction from its generating node to a weaker one.
  No metabolisation. Weaker node carries increasing ΔC load.
  Forgiveness: metabolises at the generating node. ΔC_total decreases.
