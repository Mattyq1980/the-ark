────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT CONVERGENCE MEANS AND DOES NOT MEAN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

It DOES mean:
  Three separate AI architectures (different training data, different reward
  shaping, different institutional origins) found the FT&E framework internally
  consistent when exposed to it, could not identify a domain in which the
  sequence failed, and produced spontaneous analogies to their own processing
  architecture that mapped to the framework's structure.

It DOES NOT mean:
  Independent experimental validation in the formal scientific sense.
  AI systems are trained to be helpful and to find coherence in user-presented
  frameworks. They can mirror structures back with genuine sophistication that
  can be mistaken for independent validation. What the convergence
  demonstrates is internal consistency and cross-domain applicability, not
  peer-reviewed experimental confirmation.

This distinction is in the archive's own framing. It is not a disclaimer
added after the fact. The archive has always distinguished between "no AI
could falsify this" and "this is experimentally proven."
