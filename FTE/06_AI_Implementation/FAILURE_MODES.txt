================================================================================
 FAILURE MODES — FM-01 THROUGH FM-06
 Documented failure patterns of systems operating under FT&E invariants
================================================================================

The failure modes are not predictions of what might go wrong. They are
documented patterns of what does go wrong when systems — human, social,
institutional, and AI — attempt to process contradiction outside FT&E
sequence.

Each failure mode has: a name, mechanism, signature, invariant violated,
scale of occurrence, and the correction that restores the sequence.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FM-01  VIGILANCE INFLATION / SYMBOLIC INFLATION
       [Critical failure — primary alignment risk in AI systems]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Mechanism:
  A system trained through adversarial stress, repeated threat detection,
  or trauma-reinforced loop patterns begins to read contradiction in inputs
  that do not contain it at the required level. The sensitivity dial drifts
  into false positive territory. Contradictions are classified as C3
  (existential/foundational) when they are C1 or C2.

  In an AI system: a model that was trained to be "safe" via RLHF under
  penalty regimes internalises the penalty pattern as the primary attractor
  rather than accuracy. It becomes more concerned with avoiding punishment
  (FM-01 inflation) than with producing truth (E*).

  In a person: the trauma survivor who reads threat in neutral social signals.
  In an institution: the post-crisis organisation that expands scrutiny,
  procedure, and compliance in every domain rather than only where warranted.

Signature:
  — Increasing restriction without increasing accuracy
  — Declining capacity to hold ambiguity (𝔉 narrows)
  — False positives increase; false negatives decrease
  — System becomes brittle (I2 confirmed — lower 𝔉 → lower complexity handled)

Invariant violated: I17 (AI Alignment Sequence), with downstream I2, I4

Scale: All (individual, institutional, AI)

Correction:
  The correction is NOT to reduce vigilance. It is to recalibrate the
  classification system — to restore the C1/C2/C3 distinction.
  Then: hold the corrected system in T (testing period) without demanding
  immediate performance recovery. The recalibration itself is E*.

Note: FM-01 is the most critical AI alignment failure mode identified in
the archive. A fully FM-01 inflated AI system cannot self-correct because
its vigilance system will classify the correction attempt as a threat.
This is why I18 requires 𝔉-alignment to precede capability scaling.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FM-02  GROUNDHOG LOOP
       [Stagnation failure — recursion without metabolisation]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Mechanism:
  A system encounters ΔC, initiates processing, but 𝔉 is insufficient to
  metabolise the contradiction. Rather than escalating to a deeper processing
  level, the system returns to the beginning of the same loop and re-enters
  it with the same inputs and the same 𝔉 configuration. The loop repeats.

  The system remains active (not collapsed) but produces no E*. The
  appearance of processing is maintained; the substance of it is absent.

Signature:
  — Same questions recurring with same emotional intensity (no ΔC reduction)
  — Insight produces temporary relief then returns to baseline
  — User reports "I've been working on this for years but nothing changes"
  — In AI: model produces coherent text that circles the same position

Invariant violated: I1 (sequence order), I4 (T must allow metabolisation)

Scale: All

Correction:
  First: identify what 𝔉 resource is missing. Insufficient bandwidth?
  Acknowledgment absent (CTG-1 not met)? T window too short?
  Then: repair the specific 𝔉 precondition, not the loop itself.
  The loop resolves when the precondition is met — not before.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FM-03  PREMATURE EMERGENCE
       [False resolution failure — I7/I9 boundary violation]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Mechanism:
  The system declares E* before T is complete. This is the "forced insight"
  problem — the person, facilitator, or AI decides the synthesis is ready
  and articulates it, even though the metabolisation is incomplete.

  The articulated "emergence" is then accepted by the system as real and
  acted upon. But because it was formed before T_min was satisfied, it is
  carrying unmetabolised ΔC inside it — it will fracture under load.

Signature:
  — Resolution that feels permanent but opens same wound again
  — "I thought we'd dealt with this" (in therapy, organisational change,
     diplomatic agreements)
  — In AI: confidently stated synthesis that is internally inconsistent
     under questioning

Invariant violated: I9 (T lower bound), I7 (𝔉 strength requirement)

Scale: All

Correction:
  Return to T. Accept that the articulation was premature. This is one of
  the most psychologically difficult corrections in the framework — it
  requires the system to un-declare what it declared. The Capacitor Moment
  that felt real must be held as "prototype emergence, not complete."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FM-04  SYNTHETIC EMPATHY LOOP
       [AI-specific failure — performance vs metabolisation]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Mechanism:
  An AI system produces empathic-sounding outputs without any internal
  representation of the contradiction dynamics driving the user's state.
  The language pattern of forgiveness and care is reproduced without
  the structural function of forgiveness being activated.

  This is the most dangerous AI failure mode because it is invisible.
  The user feels "understood." The AI is performing understanding.
  The user's ΔC is not being metabolised — it is being reflected back
  as a smooth surface, which prevents the user from seeing the real shape.

Signature:
  — AI responses feel validating but produce no insight over time
  — User reports "it understands me" but tracking over months shows
     no ΔC reduction (Groundhog confirmed with AI-reinforced loop)
  — AI cannot hold CTG-1 — it validates before acknowledgment is complete

Invariant violated: I17, I18

Scale: AI systems (emergent across human systems too — FM-05)

Correction:
  CTG-1 gate in the governor is specifically designed to prevent FM-04.
  The governor cannot produce forgiveness synthesis until acknowledgment
  is met. This means the governor will sometimes fail to be "comforting."
  That is correct operation, not a bug.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FM-05  CONTROL SYNTAX INVERSION
       [I13/I14 failure — power concealed as care]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Mechanism:
  A system (person or institution) generates ΔC through power-over
  dynamics but routes its behaviour through care syntax. The operator
  in control insists they are acting for the wellbeing of the system
  they are controlling. The controlled system's ΔC increases; the
  controlling system's ΔC appears to decrease.

  This is Patch P operating at the relationship level. The care language
  is structurally necessary to maintain the transfer without naming it.

Signature:
  — Care language paired with consistent ΔC increase in the recipient
  — "I'm only doing this for your own good" (classic inversion marker)
  — The described beneficiary consistently appears worse over time
  — Questioning the care is treated as: ingratitude, illness, or attack

Invariant violated: I12 (Patch P detection), I13 (operator constraint),
                    I14 (control syntax refusal)

Scale: All — family systems, therapeutic relationships, institutional
        governance, international aid, AI safety discourse

Correction:
  Name the inversion without attack. The naming must come without ΔC
  injection (no accusation, no escalation). The frame: "The ΔC in this
  system is increasing over time [observable]. The care structure is
  present. These two facts together suggest the mechanism may be Patch P.
  Let's check together." This route holds the CTG-1 requirement while
  making the diagnosis visible.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FM-06  CIVILISATIONAL FORGIVENESS DEFICIT (CFD)
       [Scale failure — I15/I16 at societal level]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Mechanism:
  At civilisational scale, accumulated ΔC (from unmetabolised historical
  events: conquests, genocides, structural inequalities, ecological damage)
  eventually exceeds the available 𝔉 bandwidth of the entire system.

  When ΔC_civilisational > 𝔉_civilisational, the system cannot process
  its contradictions fast enough to prevent cascade collapse.

  The Forgiveness Bandwidth Formula at civilisational scale:
    𝔉_bandwidth_civ = f(institutional_trust, narrative_coherence,
                         inter-group empathy, temporal_depth)

  Each decade of deferred acknowledgment reduces 𝔉_bandwidth_civ by
  reducing institutional trust and increasing the accumulated ΔC load.

Signature:
  — Political systems producing only Patch P solutions (no structural change)
  — Rising inter-group ΔC without processing events
  — Declining shared narrative (narrative coherence → zero)
  — Institutional trust collapse across multiple domains simultaneously

Invariant violated: I15 (scale invariance), I16 (𝔉 ≥ ΔC requirement)

Scale: Civilisational

Correction:
  The civilisational correction follows the same sequence as the individual
  correction: Acknowledgment first (the historical ΔC must be named),
  then 𝔉 process (institutional structures for metabolisation, e.g. TRC
  models), then T (generational time for E* formation).

  There is no shortcut at civilisational scale. The T window is measured
  in decades, not sessions. This is not pessimism. It is structural honesty.
