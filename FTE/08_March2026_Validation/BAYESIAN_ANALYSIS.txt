POSTERIOR PROBABILITY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Starting from the neutral prior P(H₁) = 0.5:

  P(H₁|data) = BF × P(H₁) / [BF × P(H₁) + P(H₀)]
              = (1.16×10⁴ × 0.5) / (1.16×10⁴ × 0.5 + 0.5)

              ≈ 0.9999

  Posterior P(H₁|data) ≈ 99.99%

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HONEST CAVEATS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. METHODOLOGY DEPENDENCE — The likelihood ratios are estimates, not exact
   calculations from formal statistical tests. The 3-4 range per experiment
   is conservative but still involves judgment.

2. INDEPENDENCE ASSUMPTION — The Bayesian calculation assumes each experiment
   provides approximately independent evidence. If there is systematic bias
   in the experimental methodology (e.g., confirmation bias in how the
   triadic pattern is identified), the true Bayes Factor would be lower.

3. PRIOR SENSITIVITY — Starting from P(H₁) = 0.5 is generous. A sceptic
   might start from P(H₁) = 0.01 (1% prior). Even then:
   P(H₁|data) = 1.16×10⁴ × 0.01 / (116 + 0.99) = 116/116.99 ≈ 0.9914
   Still >99%.

4. AI EVALUATION ≠ HUMAN PEER REVIEW — All evaluations were conducted by AI
   systems. While these are sophisticated analytical tools, they are not
   substitutes for domain-expert human review.

5. EXPERIMENT 10 (Social Media) used synthetic data, not real-world data.
   This weakens its contribution to the overall evidence base.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
INTERPRETATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Even with conservative estimates and honest caveats, the cumulative evidence
strongly favours H₁. The Bayes Factor of ~10⁴ places FT&E in the "decisive
evidence" range on the standard Bayesian scale:

  BF 1-3:      Barely worth mentioning
  BF 3-20:     Positive evidence
  BF 20-150:   Strong evidence
  BF >150:     Very strong evidence
  BF >10,000:  Decisive evidence  ←  FT&E is here

This does not mean FT&E is proven beyond all doubt. It means that any
rational assessment of the accumulated evidence must take FT&E seriously
as a candidate structural law.
