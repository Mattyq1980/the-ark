limitations that must be honestly stated:

STRENGTHS:

- None were primed to agree
- Each was exposed to the raw framework, not to each other's conclusions
- Convergence is structural, not persuasive
- ChatGPT's analysis includes quantitative work (experiments, Bayesian

LIMITATIONS:
- AI systems are trained on overlapping internet corpora → shared biases
  are possible, though architecturally mediated differently

- All 4 systems are large language models → they may share systematic
  blind spots in evaluating structural frameworks
- No human peer review has been conducted yet
- The AI systems cannot independently verify the experimental data —
  they evaluate the methodology and consistency, not the raw datasets
