1. THE COUNTEREXAMPLE
================================================================================

Source: Taylor, S.E. & Brown, J.D. (1988). "Illusion and well-being:
A social psychological perspective on mental health." Psychological
Bulletin, 103(2), 193–210.

Confirmed by: Alloy, L.B. & Abramson, L.Y. (1979). "Judgment of
contingency in depressed and nondepressed students: Sadder but
wiser?" Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 108(4), 441–485.

Findings:
  - People with mildly inaccurate positive self-beliefs show BETTER
    mental health, motivation, persistence, and social outcomes
  - Depressive realism: people with accurate self-perception show
    WORSE outcomes (more depression, less persistence)
  - Three illusion categories: unrealistic self-evaluation,
    exaggerated personal control, unrealistic optimism

The charge against FT&E:
  If belief-that-contradicts-reality is a Firewall (suppressed ΔC),
  then Taylor & Brown shows Firewalls producing BETTER outcomes.
  This would violate the prediction that suppressed ΔC accumulates
  and harms the carrier.

Critical constraint (from Taylor's own later work, 1998, 2003):
  Illusions are adaptive ONLY when MODEST and FLEXIBLE.
  Rigid, extreme positive illusions predict narcissism, poor
  learning, and relationship breakdown.
  The benefit window is narrow: 10–20% inflation, not more.

2. RESOLUTION — THREE STRUCTURAL ARGUMENTS
================================================================================

ARGUMENT 1: BANDWIDTH BUFFER (M2 sensitivity tuning)

  E* = 𝔉·T − ΔC

  A person with perfectly accurate self-perception experiences
  EVERY mismatch between self and environment as ΔC. Every failure,
  every gap, every imperfection registers as contradiction-to-process.

  For finite processing capacity 𝔉:
    If ΔC_input is very high (everything registers), 𝔉 may be
    overwhelmed: 𝔉·T < ΔC → E* < 0 (depression, withdrawal)

  A mild positive illusion REDUCES ΔC_input by ~10–20%.
  This brings ΔC below the 𝔉·T threshold → E* > 0.

  The illusion is NOT suppressing ΔC. It is FILTERING ΔC input
  to a processable level. This is the same function as a bandwidth
  limiter — not denial, but triage.

  In FT&E terms: the illusion operates at M2 (input parsing).
  It adjusts the SENSITIVITY of contradiction detection downward
  just enough that the system can process what it detects.

  Maps to I6 (Carrier Conservation): the system must maintain
  its own integrity to continue processing. A system detecting
  ALL ΔC simultaneously with no filtering crashes (depression).
  Input gating is structurally necessary for survival.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ARGUMENT 2: DOSE-RESPONSE LIFECYCLE (Taylor's own data)

  Taylor's later research constrains the benefit:
    Mild illusions:    adaptive    (10–20% inflation)
    Moderate illusions: mixed
    Extreme illusions:  maladaptive (narcissism, rigidity)

  FT&E predicts this exact pattern:
    Mild:    bandwidth buffer, ΔC stays manageable, E* > 0
    Extreme: Firewall — large ΔC gap between belief and reality,
             processing stops, accumulation, eventual collapse

  The dose-response curve matches:
    Low M2 sensitivity  → system overwhelm (depressive realism)
    Moderate sensitivity → system thrives (positive illusions)
    High insensitivity   → system rigidifies (narcissism, denial)

  This is the Goldilocks zone of M2 sensitivity. The framework
  doesn't say "detect everything." It says "process everything
  DETECTED." How much to detect is an engineering parameter.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ARGUMENT 3: DEPRESSIVE REALISM REFRAME

  Depressive realism is NOT evidence that accuracy is harmful.
  It is evidence that UNFILTERED accuracy without sufficient 𝔉
  overwhelms the processing system.

  The depressed person sees accurately but CANNOT PROCESS what
  they see. Their 𝔉·T is insufficient for their ΔC load.

  Solutions within FT&E:
    Increase 𝔉 (therapy, support, skill-building)
    Increase T (patience, reduced demands, processing time)
    Or: reduce ΔC_input (positive illusions — the cheap option)

  This works short/medium term. Long term, unprocessed ΔC accumulates
  behind the filter — explaining why extreme illusions become pathological.

3. NEW DISTINCTION: BELIEF-AS-BANDWIDTH vs BELIEF-AS-FIREWALL
================================================================================

The counterexample forced this distinction to become explicit.

BELIEF-AS-BANDWIDTH (functions as M2 sensitivity tuning):
  - Mild, flexible, adjusts when contradicting evidence is strong
  - Reduces ΔC input to processable level
  - System CONTINUES to process detected contradictions
  - Example: "I'm pretty good at this" (when actually average)
  - Outcome: sustained engagement, E* > 0

BELIEF-AS-FIREWALL (functions as suppressed ΔC):
  - Rigid, defended, does NOT adjust with evidence
  - BLOCKS specific ΔC from entering processing pipeline
  - System STOPS processing in the defended domain
  - Example: "I am NEVER wrong" (despite overwhelming evidence)
  - Outcome: accumulation, eventual collapse, E* < 0

DIAGNOSTIC CRITERION:
  Does the belief adjust when contradicting evidence exceeds
  the bandwidth buffer threshold?
    YES → bandwidth / M2 tuning → adaptive
    NO  → Firewall / suppressed ΔC → accumulating

Taylor & Brown illusions: mild, flexible, adjust under strong
evidence. PASS as bandwidth. Not Firewalls.

Narcissistic grandiosity: rigid, defended, does not adjust under
overwhelming evidence. FAIL. Firewall.

Same surface structure (positive self-belief). Opposite structural
function (bandwidth vs Firewall). Distinguishable by the diagnostic.

4. COST AND HONESTY
================================================================================

WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE FRAMEWORK:
  M2 must have an explicit SENSITIVITY PARAMETER that can be tuned.
    Too sensitive (detect all) → system overwhelm (depression)
    Too insensitive (detect none) → Firewall (narcissism, denial)
    Optimal range exists and is empirically observable.

  This was not explicit in the framework before this counterexample
  forced it. It was implicit in I6 (Carrier Conservation) and in the
  M2 classification system, but never stated as a tunable parameter.

REMAINING RISK:
  The bandwidth/Firewall distinction could become a definitional
  escape hatch — any contradicting case reclassified post hoc.

  Prevention: the diagnostic criterion (does it adjust under
  sufficient evidence?) is empirically testable and not retroactive.
  A belief is bandwidth if it yields to strong enough contradiction.
  A belief is Firewall if it persists regardless. Observable, testable.

TESTABLE PREDICTIONS FROM THE RESOLUTION:
  P1: Mild positive illusions should correlate with maintained
      engagement with contradicting feedback (bandwidth → still
      processing, just filtered)
  P2: Extreme illusions should correlate with avoidance or dismissal
      of contradicting feedback (Firewall → ΔC blocked)
  P3: The transition point from adaptive to maladaptive illusion
      should be predictable from individual 𝔉 capacity — people
      with higher processing capacity can tolerate less illusion
      (they don't need the bandwidth buffer as much)
