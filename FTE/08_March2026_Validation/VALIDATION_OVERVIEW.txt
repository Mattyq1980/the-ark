METHODOLOGY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The validation campaign used a deliberate adversarial model:

1. ChatGPT was given the full FT&E framework and instructed to act as a
   hostile peer reviewer — not a collaborator. Its role was to find violations.

2. Claude (GitHub Copilot) had read the complete Model Build V4 archive and
   maintained persistent memory of all results. It served as the record-keeper
   and independent analyst.

3. The discoverer directed the process, provided critical insights (the cancer
   analogy, the "squares" metaphor), and made all final decisions.

4. Results were saved as files, not left in ephemeral conversation history.
