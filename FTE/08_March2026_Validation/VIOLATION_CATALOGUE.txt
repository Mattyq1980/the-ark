ASSESSMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The highest-confidence "violation" is random number generators at 40%, and
even that is acknowledged as a mapping weakness (metaphorical stretch) rather
than a structural failure.

The critical insight from this catalogue is the stochastic extension:
dS = F(S)dt + αC dt + σ dW. This transforms FT&E from a deterministic
threshold model into a probabilistic one — necessary for quantum mechanics
and biology. This is not a weakness; it's a maturation of the formalism.
