  First Noble Truth: Dukkha (suffering exists)
  [SIGNAL]
    "Birth is dukkha, aging is dukkha, illness is dukkha, death
    is dukkha; union with what is displeasing is dukkha; separation

    dukkha."  — Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta (SN 56.11)

    FT&E decode: ΔC EXISTS. This is the foundational diagnosis.
    Dukkha is not "life is sad." It is the systematic gap between
    what is and what the system insists should be. Every item in
    the list is a contradiction between expectation and reality.

    A mechanical description of accumulated ΔC.

    P1 CONFIRMED: ΔC accumulation is the foundational diagnosis.

  Second Noble Truth: Samudaya (origin of dukkha)
  [SIGNAL]
    "It is this craving (tanha) which leads to renewed existence,
    accompanied by delight and lust, seeking delight here and
    there; that is, craving for sensual pleasures, craving for
    existence, craving for extermination."
    — Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta (SN 56.11)

    FT&E decode: This is the MECHANISM of ΔC accumulation.
    Tanha (craving) is not the ΔC itself — it is the process

    The system doesn't just have ΔC — it PRODUCES ΔC continuously

    The desire to DESTROY what contradicts you. This is Patch P

  Third Noble Truth: Nirodha (cessation of dukkha)
  [SIGNAL]
