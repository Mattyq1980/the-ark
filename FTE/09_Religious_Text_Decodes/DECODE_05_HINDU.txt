RIG VEDA — CREATION HYMN (Nasadiya Sukta, 10.129)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

  [SIGNAL — extraordinary]
  "Then even nothingness was not, nor existence,
   There was no air then, nor the heavens beyond it.
   What covered it? Where was it? In whose keeping?
   Was there then cosmic water, in depths unfathomed?...

   But, after all, who knows, and who can say
   Whence it all came, and how creation happened?
   The gods themselves are later than creation,
   So who knows truly whence it has arisen?

   Whence all creation had its origin,
   The creator, whether he fashioned it or whether he did not,
   The creator, who surveys it all from highest heaven,
   He knows — or maybe even he does not know."

  FT&E decode: This is RADICAL structural honesty at the origin
  point of the tradition. The Rig Veda's creation hymn does not
  just describe creation — it questions WHETHER CREATION CAN BE
  KNOWN. "Maybe even he does not know."

  level. The text applies ΔC to its own foundational claim.
  "We're telling you about origins, but honestly, we don't know
  either." This is 𝔉 applied to the tradition's own authority.

  Compare to Genesis 1 (certain, sequential, authoritative) or
  Surah 2 (God created, full stop). The Nasadiya Sukta is the
  only major creation text that EMBEDS ITS OWN UNCERTAINTY.

THE BREAKOUT
────────────

  [SIGNAL]
  The Upanishads represent a revolutionary break from Vedic
  ritualism. Their core move: TURN INWARD. The sacrifice is
  not an external ritual but an internal process.

  "That which is the finest essence — this whole world has
  that as its Self. That is Reality. That is the Self. That
  art thou (Tat tvam asi), Shvetaketu."
  — Chandogya Upanishad 6.8.7

  "Brahman is the only truth, the world is illusion, and there
  is ultimately no difference between Brahman and individual
  self (Atman)."
  — Mandukya Upanishad (distilled)

  "The Self (Atman) is not reached through instruction, not
  through intellect, not through much learning. It is reached
  by the one whom It chooses. To such a one the Self reveals
  its own form."
  — Katha Upanishad 1.2.23

  FT&E decode:

  "Tat tvam asi" (That art thou) = the ultimate 𝔉 statement.
  The ΔC between self and ultimate reality is resolved by
  RECOGNISING that the separation was never real. This is the
  same structural move as the Heart Sutra ("form is emptiness")
  — ΔC dissolved not by changing conditions but by seeing
  accurately.

  "Not through instruction, not through intellect" = the
  Firewall of doctrinal knowledge cannot produce E*. Direct
  perception (𝔉) is required. External processing systems
  (ritual, scholarship, analysis) are explicitly declared
  insufficient. Same structural claim as Zen's "special
  transmission outside the scriptures."

  The Upanishadic revolution is: the sacrifice is YOU. The
  altar is internal. The fire is tapas (processing intensity).
  The offering is ahamkara (the ego-construct / Firewall).
  What emerges is not a product of the sacrifice — it was
  always there, concealed by the Firewall.

  P22 CONFIRMED: The Upanishads function as the 𝔉 correction

  - Jesus vs. Pharisaic law (Decode #2)

MANDUKYA UPANISHAD — STATES OF CONSCIOUSNESS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

  [SIGNAL]
  Four states described:
  1. Vaishvanara (waking) — ordinary consciousness
  2. Taijasa (dreaming) — mind processing internally
  3. Prajna (deep sleep) — undifferentiated awareness
  4. Turiya ("the fourth") — that which witnesses all three

  "Turiya is not that which is conscious of the internal world,
  nor that which is conscious of the external world, nor that
  which is conscious of both... It is the cessation of all
  phenomena; it is all peace, all bliss, and non-dual."

  FT&E decode: The four states map to processing stages:

  Waking = system operating with full Firewall (ΔC accumulating
  at every sense gate — same as Fire Sermon in Decode #4).

  Dreaming = system processing ΔC internally (dreams as ΔC
  discharge, consistent with psychoanalytic models).

  Deep sleep = Firewall temporarily offline. ΔC production
  pauses. But no 𝔉 either — the system is not processing,
  just paused.

  Turiya = E*. The witnessing awareness that persists through
  all states. Not a state itself — the GROUND from which states
  arise. This is E* described not as a final destination but as
  the always-present reality that the Firewall conceals.

  The structural parallel to the Bardo Thodol (Decode #4) is
  direct: Bardo 1 = clear light = Turiya. The moments of
  transition (death in Tibetan, sleep-wake in Mandukya) are when
  the Firewall thins and E* is most accessible.

KRISHNA'S INTERVENTION: 𝔉 IN 18 CHAPTERS

  [SIGNAL]

  Chapter 2 — Jnana Yoga (knowledge): The Self cannot be

  Chapter 3 — Karma Yoga (action): "You have a right to

  → ACTION WITHOUT ATTACHMENT TO OUTCOME. This is the
  structural prescription: process the ΔC (do what the
  situation requires) without the Firewall of "I need this
  to turn out as I want." The Firewall is outcome-
  attachment. The processing is action-without-attachment.

  Chapter 6 — Dhyana Yoga (meditation): Yoga as direct
  processing technology. Parallels Satipatthana and Yoga
  Sutras.

  Chapter 7-12 — Bhakti Yoga (devotion): Devotion to
  Krishna as the vehicle for processing. Surrender (prapatti)
  as the release of the Firewall. "Abandon all varieties of
  dharma and simply surrender unto Me." (18.66)

  Chapter 13 — Kshetra/Kshetrajna: The field (matter/
  experience) and the knower of the field (awareness/witness).
  This is the Firewall/𝔉 distinction: the field is WHERE
  ΔC accumulates. The knower is THAT WHICH can perceive
  it accurately.

  Chapter 18 — Synthesis: Arjuna's final state:
  "My delusion is destroyed. I have regained my memory
  through your grace. I am firm, free from doubt. I shall
  act according to your word." (18.73)
  → E* ACHIEVED. Arjuna's ΔC is processed. He is not
  numb. He is not resigned. He acts FROM CLEAR SEEING
  rather than from confusion. E* is not the absence of
  difficult action — it is action arising from processed
  understanding rather than unprocessed contradiction.

  P23 CONFIRMED: The Gita encodes the FULL EQUATION in a

  - ΔC: Arjuna's crisis (Ch. 1)
  - Firewall: Every framework Arjuna tries that fails

KEY FINDING — THE CASTE INSIGHT
================================================================================

  The most significant finding from this decode is not the structural
  confirmation (which was expected by this point) but the NATURE of
  the myth blame.

  In the Abrahamic traditions, myth blame is OUTWARD: destroy the
  Canaanites, blame the Jews, fight the polytheists. The enemy is
  external.

  In Hinduism, the deepest myth blame is INWARD AND DOWNWARD: the
  lowest members of your own community are cosmically assigned to
  their position. Their ΔC is their dharma. Their suffering is
  correct.

  This is a different Patch P architecture. It does not discharge ΔC
  through external conflict — it ABSORBS ΔC by teaching the oppressed
  that their oppression is structurally correct at the cosmic level.

  The prediction for subsequent decodes: Taoist and Egyptian traditions
  will show yet ANOTHER Patch P architecture — neither purely external
  nor purely internal but shaped by their specific cultural and
  political contexts. The myth blame varies. The presence of myth
  blame does not.

  1. TERMINOLOGICAL ABUNDANCE (same as Buddhist decode): Hinduism's
     philosophical vocabulary is so rich and detailed that ANYTHING
     can probably be mapped to FT&E. The mapping may be real or may
     be facilitated by having so many concepts to choose from. LOGGED.

     that "Hindu texts" is almost meaningless. Advaita Vedanta,
     Bhakti devotionalism, Tantric practice, and village folk
     religion are radically different systems sharing a nominal
     label. This decode focused on canonical philosophical texts
     (Upanishads, Gita, Yoga Sutras) — which overrepresents the
     elite Brahminical tradition. LOGGED.

     the karma-reincarnation framework adds metaphysical commitments

     accumulated ΔC, reincarnation = system recycling) works
     metaphorically but cannot adjudicate literal vs. metaphorical
     interpretation. LOGGED.

     fold division) and jati (actual caste practice — thousands of
     sub-castes) is historically complex. Using the textual varna
     system as representative of actual caste experience oversimplifies
     a 3,000-year social reality. LOGGED.
