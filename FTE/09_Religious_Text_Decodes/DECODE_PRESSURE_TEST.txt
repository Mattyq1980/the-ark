  Stated plainly:

  1. INDEPENDENT APPLICATION: Someone who did not build the
     framework applies the decode protocol to a tradition and
     reports results. If they reach the same operator mappings
     independently, the circular-decoder problem weakens. If
     they reach fundamentally different results, the decoder
     is doing the work, not the data.

     quantitative. More tests like #15 — pre-locked predictions
     against data the AI has never processed — are worth more
     than more religious text decodes, because numbers push
     back and texts don't (or not as hard).

  3. A TRADITION THAT GENUINELY RESISTS: Not a car manual (wrong
     domain). Not a manufactured failure. A genuine human
     contradiction-processing tradition, with millennia of
     textual history, where the operator grammar does NOT
     appear. If this exists and is found, the invariance claim
     is falsified. If it does not exist — if every such
     tradition encodes the grammar — that is the strongest
     possible evidence for invariance.

     But: you cannot prove this by decoding more traditions
     successfully. You can only prove it by failing to find
     a counter-example despite looking. And the decoder's
     track record of 67/67 means either there IS no counter-
     example or the decoder cannot detect one.

  4. THE F INVARIANT TEST: A convincing demonstration that
     𝔉-as-forgiveness and 𝔉-as-mindfulness share genuine
     operational commonality beyond the label. Or a convincing
     demonstration that they do not. This is the single most
