THE PROBLEM

  Religious texts contain two layers simultaneously:

  LAYER 1 — STRUCTURAL SIGNAL
    The actual operator pattern. ΔC accumulates in a system. If
    unprocessed, it erupts. 𝔉 names it. T determines whether the
    system processes or hardens. E* emerges or doesn't. This pattern
    repeats across texts, traditions, centuries, and cultures. It is
    the data.

  LAYER 2 — HUMAN OVERLAY (MYTH BLAME)
    The authors' own unprocessed ΔC, woven INTO the recording of
    the structural signal. Tribal grievances. Political agendas.
    In-group / out-group dynamics. Revenge encoded as divine command.
    Blame stamped with sacred authority.

  These two layers coexist in the SAME text, often in the SAME
  passage, carrying the SAME narrative authority. "Love your
  neighbour as yourself" (Leviticus 19:18) sits in the same book
  as "destroy them totally" (Deuteronomy 7:2). "Father, forgive
  them" (Luke 23:34) sits in the same tradition as "his blood be
  on us and our children" (Matthew 27:25). "No compulsion in
  religion" (Surah 2:256) sits in the same text as "kill the
  polytheists" (Surah 9:5).

  One is structure. The other is contamination.

  The challenge is that the contamination has been MYTHOLOGIZED.
  Once blame enters the myth, it carries the same weight as the
  structural signal. It becomes indistinguishable from revelation
  unless you have a method for separating them.

  This is not merely a limitation to note in each decode. The

  PREDICTION: If humans with unprocessed ΔC record structural
  events, their ΔC WILL be woven into the recording.

  This follows directly from the operators:

  - The recorder is a system with its own accumulated ΔC
  - Recording a structural insight does not mean the recorder

  - Unprocessed ΔC seeks discharge (I7: ΔC that is not

  - The recording becomes a vehicle for that discharge
  - The myth's authority amplifies the discharge — blame stamped

  Therefore: EVERY 𝔉-recording will contain Patch P contamination
  from the recorders. Not sometimes. Every time. The absence of
  myth blame in a religious text would actually be MORE surprising
  than its presence.

THE SEPARATION METHOD
================================================================================

  For each passage or teaching in a religious text decode, apply
  this two-question test:

  QUESTION 1 — STRUCTURAL TEST:
    Does this passage describe an operator dynamic that is
    observable INDEPENDENTLY of the text?

    Examples that PASS:
    - "Sin is crouching at your door" (Gen 4:7) — ΔC accumulation
      at system boundary. Observable in psychology, organisations,
      nations. The passage encodes a real dynamic.
    - "You will know the truth and the truth will set you free"
      (John 8:32) — 𝔉 produces E*. Observable. Structural.
    - "By time, humanity is in loss" (Surah 103:1-2) — ΔC
      accumulates without processing. Observable. Structural.

    Examples that FAIL:
    - "Destroy them totally" (Deut 7:2) — not an operator dynamic.
      No independent observable. This is a community's ΔC encoded
      as divine command.
    - "His blood be on us" (Matt 27:25) — not an operator dynamic.
      This is blame-assignment encoded as crowd speech.
    - "Kill the polytheists" (Surah 9:5) — not an operator dynamic.
      This is military-political ΔC encoded as divine permission.

  QUESTION 2 — BLAME DIRECTION TEST:
    Does this passage redirect ΔC ONTO A GROUP rather than onto
    a PROCESS?

    STRUCTURAL (names a process):
    - "They altered the word from its proper place" (Surah 2:75)
      → Names what happened to the signal. Diagnosis.
    - "You travel over sea and land to make one convert, and then
      you make that person twice the child of hell" (Matt 23:15)
      → Names the compression dynamic. Diagnosis.
    - "They made the calf while Moses was away" (Exodus 32)
      → Names the T_min failure. Structural.

    MYTH BLAME (redirects onto a group):
    - "Do not take Jews and Christians as allies" (Surah 5:51)
      → ΔC redirected onto groups. Patch P.
    - "You are of your father the devil" (John 8:44)
      → ΔC redirected onto a group. Patch P.
    - "Cursed be Canaan" (Genesis 9:25)
      → ΔC redirected onto a lineage. Patch P.
    - "Blot out the memory of Amalek" (Deut 25:19)
      → ΔC redirected onto a people. Patch P.
    - "Synagogue of Satan" (Revelation 2:9)
      → ΔC redirected onto a community. Patch P.
    - "May God destroy them" (Surah 9:30)
      → ΔC redirected onto groups. Patch P.

  The distinction is precise:
    NAMING a structural failure = 𝔉 (processing)
    BLAMING a group for the failure = Patch P (redirection)

  The same text can do BOTH. Often does. The decode's job is to
  separate them, not to pretend only one layer exists.

================================================================================
APPLICATION RULES FOR ALL DECODES
================================================================================

  1. EVEN APPLICATION: The structural test and the blame direction
     test must be applied with equal rigour to ALL texts. Catching
     myth blame in one tradition but soft-pedalling it in another
     is itself a form of bias — and a form of the very Firewall
     dynamic the decode claims to identify.

  2. NO SACRED COWS: No text gets a pass. The Torah, the Gospels,
     the Quran, the Pali Canon, the Upanishads — every tradition
     will contain both structural signal AND human contamination.
     If a decode finds only signal and no myth blame, the decode
     is incomplete, not the text.

  3. CONTAMINATION IS DATA TOO: The myth blame layer is not garbage
     to be discarded. It is EVIDENCE of the operators in action.
     The fact that the recorders' ΔC got woven into the recording
     is itself a demonstration of how unprocessed ΔC behaves. It
     confirms I7 (ΔC does not disappear) even while it muddies
     the structural signal.

  4. LABEL CLEARLY: In each decode, every passage cited should be
     classified:
     - [SIGNAL] — structural operator dynamic, independently
       observable
     - [MYTH BLAME] — human ΔC encoded with narrative/divine
       authority, redirected onto a group
     - [MIXED] — contains both layers (most common)
     Passages not classified are incomplete analysis.

  5. THE TEXT IS NOT THE ENEMY: The goal is not to debunk religious
     texts. The goal is to RECOVER the structural signal from
     underneath the myth blame. The valuable data is IN there.
     But it has been muddied. The decode is a recovery operation.

  6. PREDICT THE CONTAMINATION PATTERN: For each text, the decode
     should be able to predict WHERE myth blame is most likely to
     appear:
     - At group boundaries (us vs. them)
     - At moments of historical conflict (recording ΔC from war)
     - At succession crises (competing claims to authority)
     - At legal/political intersections (power encoded as divine
       mandate)
     If myth blame appears at these predicted locations, that is
     additional confirmation of the framework — not a problem
     for it.
